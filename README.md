## What Is SJP Ultrasonics?

SJP Ultrasonics operates in the industrial technology space, providing solutions related to **plastic joining, industrial automation and laser technology**. Its customers span sectors including automotive, medical, electronics, FMCG, packaging and defence.

The company is raising the entire issue through a fresh issue of **35 lakh shares**. The proposed listing is on the **BSE SME platform**.
